# Reporting Agencies Internal Reference

This is an internal routing reference for future report workflows. It must not
be rendered as a default destination list on the Reports page.

## Product Boundary

- Reports page: history of generated reports and follow-up state.
- Scan result: place where a user starts a report or outreach workflow.
- Developer or maintainer contact should be suggested first when the concern is
  a reproducible app or service defect and there is no urgent safety issue.
- Escalation destinations should be selected by issue class, jurisdiction, user
  choice, and current official intake requirements.
- Verify official intake URLs before surfacing a link in the product.

## Developer First

- Software developer or maintainer
- Vendor security contact
- Platform trust and safety team
- App store review/support channel
- Open source project issue tracker or security advisory intake

## United States Federal And Public Reporting Destinations

- FTC: fraud, scams, deceptive business practices, consumer protection.
- FBI IC3: cyber-enabled crime, online fraud, account compromise.
- CISA: cyber incidents, vulnerabilities, malware, phishing, infrastructure.
- IRS: tax fraud, IRS impersonation, tax identity theft, tax-related scams.
- CFPB: banking, credit, debt, payments, loans, consumer finance.
- SEC: securities fraud, investment scams, market-related tips.
- FCC: robocalls, unwanted texts, telecom, broadband, communications complaints.
- HHS OCR: health information privacy and security complaints.
- DOJ Civil Rights: civil rights violations and discrimination complaints.
- State Attorney General: state consumer protection and business complaints.
- State consumer protection office: local consumer complaints and scams.
- Local law enforcement: urgent safety, theft, harassment, or local criminal reports.
- USPS Inspection Service: mail fraud and postal service related scams.
- NHTSA: vehicle safety defects and automotive safety concerns.

## Non-Governmental And Advocacy Destinations

- BBB: marketplace complaints, scam tracking, business response tracking.
- EFF: digital rights, privacy, surveillance, and legal assistance routing.
- Consumer Reports advocacy: consumer product safety and marketplace patterns.
- IdentityTheft.gov: identity theft recovery and FTC-backed recovery planning.

## Terminology Notes

- User-mentioned "ICS" should be clarified before product use. For cybercrime
  reporting, the likely intended destination is FBI IC3.
- This list is a routing aid, not legal advice.
- The app should preserve evidence provenance and neutral language regardless of
  destination.
