# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: January 27, 2025**

## 1. Introduction

CleanHouse is committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use CleanHouse.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Account Information
When you create a CleanHouse account, we collect:
- Full name
- Email address
- Password (hashed and encrypted)

### 2.2 Usage Information
We may collect information about how you use CleanHouse, including:
- File organization patterns
- Application usage statistics
- Error logs and diagnostic information

### 2.3 OAuth Information
When you sign in with Google or Apple:
- We receive basic profile information (name, email)
- We do not receive or store your OAuth provider passwords
- OAuth providers have their own privacy policies

## 3. How We Use Your Information

### 3.1 Primary Uses
We use your information to:
- Provide and maintain CleanHouse services
- Authenticate your identity
- Improve CleanHouse functionality
- Provide customer support

### 3.2 Data Processing
- All data processing occurs locally on your device
- We do not transmit personal data to third parties without your explicit consent
- Your files and data remain under your control

## 4. Data Storage and Security

### 4.1 Local Storage
- Your data is primarily stored locally on your device
- Account information is stored in a secure local database
- Passwords are hashed using industry-standard bcrypt encryption

### 4.2 Security Measures
We implement security measures including:
- Password hashing with bcrypt
- Secure local database storage
- No transmission of sensitive data to external servers
- Regular security updates

### 4.3 Data Retention
- Account information is retained until you delete your account
- Usage data may be retained for service improvement purposes
- You can request deletion of your data at any time

## 5. Information Sharing

### 5.1 No Third-Party Sharing
CleanHouse does **not**:
- Sell your personal information
- Share your data with third parties for marketing purposes
- Transmit your files or personal data to external servers

### 5.2 Limited Sharing
We may share information only in these limited circumstances:
- With your explicit consent
- To comply with legal obligations
- To protect our rights and prevent fraud

## 6. Your Rights and Choices

### 6.1 Access and Control
You have the right to:
- Access your personal information
- Correct inaccurate information
- Delete your account and data
- Export your data

### 6.2 Account Management
You can:
- Update your account information through the application
- Change your password at any time
- Delete your account and all associated data

### 6.3 Data Portability
You can export your data in a standard format for use with other applications.

## 7. Cookies and Tracking

### 7.1 Local Storage
CleanHouse may use local storage to:
- Remember your preferences
- Maintain your session
- Store application settings

### 7.2 No External Tracking
We do not use:
- Third-party cookies
- External analytics services
- Cross-site tracking

## 8. Children's Privacy

CleanHouse is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## 9. International Users

### 9.1 Data Location
- Your data is stored locally on your device
- No international data transfers occur
- You maintain full control over your data

### 9.2 Compliance
CleanHouse is designed to comply with:
- General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)
- California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA)
- Other applicable privacy laws

## 10. Changes to This Policy

### 10.1 Updates
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by:
- Posting the updated policy in the application
- Sending a notification through the application
- Updating the "Last Updated" date

### 10.2 Continued Use
Your continued use of CleanHouse after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.

## 11. Contact Information

### 11.1 Privacy Questions
If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us through:
- The CleanHouse application support system
- Our official support channels

### 11.2 Data Requests
To exercise your privacy rights, please use the account management features in CleanHouse or contact our support team.

## 12. Security Breaches

### 12.1 Notification
In the unlikely event of a security breach affecting your data, we will:
- Notify you as soon as possible
- Provide details about the breach
- Explain steps taken to address the issue

### 12.2 Prevention
We implement security measures to prevent breaches, including:
- Regular security audits
- Secure coding practices
- Local data storage to minimize exposure

## 13. Third-Party Services

### 13.1 OAuth Providers
When using Google or Apple sign-in:
- You are subject to their privacy policies
- We only receive basic profile information
- We do not control their data practices

### 13.2 No Third-Party Analytics
We do not use third-party analytics services that would track your usage across other websites or applications.

---

**By using CleanHouse, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to this Privacy Policy.**

**Your privacy and data security are our top priorities. CleanHouse is designed to keep your data local and under your control.**
