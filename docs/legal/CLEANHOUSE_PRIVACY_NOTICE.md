CleanHouse Privacy Notice

Last Updated: October 23, 2026

---

### 1. Scope

This Notice explains how CleanHouse handles information when you use the product. CleanHouse is local-first: core product data is kept in the storage assigned to the installation you control. Optional connected services are used only when you configure or select them.

### 2. Information CleanHouse handles

Depending on enabled features and permissions, CleanHouse may handle:

* account identifiers, authentication-provider links, device sessions, MFA factors, and policy receipts;
* application, process, network, filesystem, configuration, and security-event evidence;
* findings, allowed-threat decisions, mediation requests, execution receipts, and audit history;
* settings, notification preferences, and diagnostic information; and
* files or records you explicitly select for inspection, preservation, import, or export.

MFA secrets are encrypted at rest. Recovery codes are shown once and stored only as one-way hashes. CleanHouse does not store your OAuth-provider password.

### 3. Why information is used

CleanHouse uses this information to authenticate you, enforce consent and security boundaries, operate requested security features, display evidence, preserve receipts, recover from failures, and provide support or diagnostics you explicitly request.

CleanHouse does not sell personal information or use private product data to train general-purpose AI models.

### 4. Local storage

“Local” means the storage assigned to the current CleanHouse installation. It may be a personal device, organization-managed machine, or another runtime controlled by the user or organization. Capacity and retention depend on that environment and are not automatically cloud storage.

Local records may include durable security evidence and append-only receipts. Closing the browser or signing out does not delete those records.

### 5. Optional connected services

CleanHouse may communicate with a service only when required for a feature you configure or request, such as:

* OAuth identity providers for sign-in;
* an email provider for magic links or account recovery;
* security-data or notification providers;
* a backup or export destination; or
* VVAULT for separately selected preservation or continuity features.

VVAULT enrollment is optional and separate. CleanHouse does not create a VVAULT account, accept VVAULT policies, transmit data to VVAULT, or open its dashboard unless you explicitly choose the relevant action. Each connected provider processes information under its own terms and privacy notice.

### 6. Sharing

CleanHouse shares information only as necessary to complete an action you request, operate a provider you configured, comply with law, protect users or systems, or support a transaction or organization that lawfully controls the installation. The product should identify the relevant destination before an optional transfer.

### 7. Retention and control

Retention depends on the record type, configured storage, integrity requirements, and applicable law. You may use available controls to export, archive, restore, amend, revoke, or delete supported information. Some audit and receipt records are append-only so that prior security actions cannot be silently rewritten.

### 8. Security

CleanHouse uses safeguards appropriate to its local-first design, including authenticated sessions, origin and CSRF checks, protected credentials, encrypted MFA secrets, scoped authorization, and auditable receipts. No system can guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for device security, backups, provider accounts, and recovery material.

### 9. Your choices and rights

You may decline updated policies and remain locked out of protected features, disconnect optional providers, manage authentication factors, sign out, and use supported data-management controls. Applicable law may provide additional access, correction, deletion, portability, restriction, or objection rights.

### 10. Contact

Privacy questions or requests may be sent to [info@thewreck.org](mailto:info@thewreck.org).
