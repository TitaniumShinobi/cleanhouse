CleanHouse Privacy Notice

Last Updated: November 9, 2025

⸻

### 1. We Don’t Want Your Data

CleanHouse is built to work **without collecting, retaining, or analyzing your personal data**. It’s a cleanup utility — not a surveillance device. We designed it to minimize digital residue, not generate more.

⸻

### 2. What We Temporarily Handle

To perform its core function, CleanHouse may briefly interact with:

* **Local system paths**
* **Volatile memory states**
* **Temporary session logs (non-identifiable)**

All such data is used **ephemerally** and discarded immediately after process completion. CleanHouse **does not transmit** this data to any cloud server or third party.

⸻

### 3. What We Don’t Collect

We do **not** collect, store, or retain:

* Personally identifiable information (PII)
* Files, folders, or directory contents
* IP addresses or location data
* Biometric or behavioral fingerprints
* Usage analytics, telemetry, or crash reports (unless manually submitted)

---

### 4. No AI Training or Profiling

CleanHouse is a **non-consumptive utility**. That means:

* Your device activity is **never used to train machine learning models**
* No behavioral data is extracted, sold, or processed
* There is **no profiling** — no identifiers, no tracking cookies, no surveillance

⸻

### 5. Local-Only Mode by Default

CleanHouse runs **entirely on your machine**, unless you explicitly enable cloud sync (optional). If enabled:

* Cloud sync encrypts all data **end-to-end**
* Decryption keys are held **only by you**
* CleanHouse servers perform **zero processing or inspection**

⸻

### 6. Optional Crash Logs

If you choose to submit a crash log:

* You may review its contents before sending
* Logs are used strictly for bug fixing
* Logs are never shared beyond internal engineering

⸻

### 7. Data Sovereignty & Control

All control remains with **you**:

* You decide what’s cleaned, kept, or excluded
* Nothing is auto-deleted without explicit confirmation
* You can purge your CleanHouse history at any time
