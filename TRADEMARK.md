# CleanHouse trademark policy

The GPL license grants rights in the software code. It does not grant rights
to use the CleanHouse name, logos, trade dress, or other source identifiers in
a way that suggests sponsorship, endorsement, or origin.

Unmodified redistribution may truthfully identify the software as CleanHouse.
Publicly distributed modified versions that could be confused with the
official product must use a different product name and visual identity and
must describe their relationship as a fork. Accurate nominative references,
copyright notices, and compatibility statements remain permitted.

This policy is a project statement pending final legal review; it is not a
restriction on exercising the GPL rights in the covered code.
